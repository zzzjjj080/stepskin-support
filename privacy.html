Privacy Policy

Stepskin

What Stepskin collects

Nothing. Stepskin has no servers and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Health data

Stepskin reads your step count from Health (HealthKit) and from the step counter on your Apple Watch, only to show it on your watch face and in the app. The number stays on your devices. It is never sent off the device, never sold and never used for advertising.

Settings

Your chosen look and your step goal are stored on your iPhone and Apple Watch and are sent between your own two devices so both show the same thing. They are not sent anywhere else.

No network

Stepskin does not connect to the internet at all. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking.
